Settings
Column mapping, thresholds and backup
Plants
SI10 is closed and acts as a warehouse only; SI11 is the running plant. Usage averages for 7 and 30 days use SI11 movements only, the all-time average uses both.
Thresholds & behaviour
Usage rates are per day the machines run, and projections draw no stock on the days they do not. Consumption posted at a weekend still counts towards the total — it is only the divisor that changes.
SAP exports normally carry signed quantities (receipts positive, issues negative). Auto-detect trusts those signs when it finds negatives, and otherwise applies the sign implied by the movement type.
Column mapping
Set once from your first files and reused for every later upload. Fields marked * are required.
Dropbox sync
Keeps the database in a single file in your Dropbox so any computer that connects here works from the same numbers. Uploads are compressed and reconciled against what is already in the cloud, so nothing is silently overwritten.
